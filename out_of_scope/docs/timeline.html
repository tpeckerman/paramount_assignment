When each rule bites, over 24 hours

Built from the rule table, not drawn by hand. Every bar means the same thing: the content may be transmitted then. A prohibition is shown as the hours that remain, so there is one reading of the chart rather than two. Choosing what is depicted narrows to the rules for that content plus the framework that applies whatever is on screen, marked applies to all content: the watershed, the definitions it rests on, and the access controls that can lift it. The day runs 05:30 to 05:30 rather than midnight to midnight, because the UK watershed is 21:00 to 05:30 and a midnight axis splits it into two fragments at opposite edges that read as two separate permissions. Rules that permit no hour at all, and rules whose restriction is not a time, are listed underneath rather than drawn at an invented position.
