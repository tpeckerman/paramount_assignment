# Not used by this project

Downloaded in good faith, then set aside. Kept rather than deleted so the next person
can see what was considered and why it was excluded, instead of wondering whether it
was missed.

## Out of scope: Code Sections Five to Ten and their guidance

Sections One, Two, Three and Four are in scope; Five to Ten are not. The reason is in
`SCOPE_DECISION.md` and it is NOT that they do not apply - the news and commercial
sections genuinely bind this broadcaster. It is that they yield no age rating and no
broadcast hour, so they belong to a different compliance workflow.

If the scope ever widens to advertising or sponsorship, Section Nine and its guidance
are the first things to pick up, because a preschool brand makes the
children's-programming restrictions live.

## One near-miss worth knowing about

`broadcast-code-guidance-section-6-march-2017.pdf` is guidance for Section SIX
(Elections and Referendums), "Issue Six: 22 March 2017". An automated substring check
matched it to UK-G2, which is guidance for Section ONE, "Issue Six: 30 March 2015".
The only thing the two share is the coincidental words "Issue Six". That false match is
why the manifest's expect strings were rewritten to be document-identifying.
